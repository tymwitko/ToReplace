# ToReplace

ToReplace is a tasklist for chores you need to do in intervals.
Did you know that you should clean your dishwasher every 3 months - and your washing machine every 6 months?
Oh, and you should also replace your water filters every 30 days.
And that toothbrush of yours?
Replace it every 3 months!

There are so many things to maintain on a schedule - but how do you remember when you last did them?
That's where _ToReplace_ comes in!

## Tasks
Every task you add requires you to enter an interval (e.g. 6 months) and the date of the last time you did it (don't worry, we won't judge, it's all offline anyway).
On the main screen you'll see how much time you've got left (if any) until you should perform your duties again.
Once you complete your task, you may click the "DONE" button to reset the countdown.
You can also reset the countdown before it's due, in case you're _that_ diligent.
